OMORA ADVENTURES Ltd

Bringing You Closer to Africa's Wild Beauty

Explore the Untamed. Experience the Extraordinary.

OMORA ADVENTURES Ltd is a tours, travel and car hire company committed to creating exceptional travel experiences across Kenya and beyond. We are passionate about connecting travellers with the beauty of Africa through unforgettable wildlife safaris, exciting destinations, rich cultural experiences and scenic adventures.

Whether you are planning an adventurous safari, a relaxing holiday, a family getaway, a business trip or simply looking for a reliable vehicle to explore your preferred destinations, we offer travel solutions designed around your needs. Our services combine personalized tour planning, convenient car hire and dependable ground transportation to make every journey comfortable, enjoyable and hassle-free.

At OMORA ADVENTURES, we believe that every journey should be more than just reaching a destination. It should be an opportunity to discover new places, experience different cultures, appreciate nature and create lasting memories. We are dedicated to delivering reliable services, personalized attention and memorable experiences that inspire you to explore more.
